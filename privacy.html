Privacy Policy

Game: I Transmigrated to Another World to Become the Overlord
Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This is a single-player story-driven role-playing game. We do not actively collect your personal identity information. The following data may be generated while you play:

  • Local save data — game progress, character status, items and achievement records, stored only on your own computer.
  • Achievement data — when you launch the game through the Epic Games Store and are signed in to your Epic account, your in-game achievements (16 in total) are synced to your Epic account via Epic Online Services (EOS).
  • Crash and runtime logs — only provided to us by you voluntarily when you report a problem.

2. How We Use Information

  • Local save data is used to restore your game progress.
  • Achievement data is used to display your play history on your Epic account profile.
  • We do not use any data for advertising, user profiling, or sell it to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

This game uses the following third-party services, whose data handling is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Epic Games / Epic Online Services — used for account sign-in and achievement syncing. See the Epic Privacy Policy.
  • Steam (Valve Corporation) — if you purchased the game on Steam, achievements and cloud saves are handled by Steam. See the Steam Privacy Policy Agreement.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Game saves are stored locally on your device (Windows: %APPDATA%\dreamworld\save\).
  • Achievement data is hosted in the cloud by Epic Games. You may unlink this game or delete the data at any time in your Epic account settings.
  • We do not upload your data to any server of our own.

5. AI Features

This game includes AI-powered character dialogue. Dialogue content is generated locally or through a third-party model interface. We do not use your conversation content for model training, and it is never linked to your real identity.

6. Children's Privacy

This game contains romance and combat themes and is primarily intended for adult players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are a guardian and believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the details below and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time: delete your local save files, unlink this game from your Epic account, or request that we delete any information you voluntarily provided to us.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced in-game or on the store page.

本作为单机剧情角色扮演游戏，我们不会主动收集您的个人身份信息。在您游玩过程中，可能产生以下数据：

  • 本地存档数据：游戏进度、角色状态、道具与成就记录，仅保存在您自己的电脑上。

二、信息的使用目的

  • 本地存档用于恢复您的游戏进度。
  • 成就数据用于在 Epic 账号资料页展示您的游戏历程。

本作使用了以下第三方服务，其数据处理受各自隐私政策约束：

  • Epic Games / Epic Online Services：用于账号登录与成就同步。详见 Epic 隐私政策。
  • Steam（Valve Corporation）：如您通过 Steam 平台购买，成就与云存档由 Steam 处理。详见 Steam 隐私政策。

四、数据存储与安全

  • 游戏存档保存在您本机目录（Windows：%APPDATA%\dreamworld\save\）。
