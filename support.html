Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The game won't start / crashes on launch?

A: Please try the following in order:
1. Make sure the Epic Games Launcher is updated to the latest version
2. In your Library, find this game → click "…" → Verify → retry after the repair finishes
3. Update your graphics card drivers

Q: Where are my save files? How do I back them up?

A: Saves are located at %APPDATA%\dreamworld\save\. We recommend copying the entire dreamworld folder regularly as a backup, especially before reinstalling your operating system.

Q: My achievements aren't showing up?

A: Achievements are synced to your Epic account. Open the Epic Games Launcher → click your profile picture (top right) → Profile → the Achievements tab to view your progress for this game. Syncing may take a few minutes.

Q: The game runs slowly or stutters?

A: This game contains a large number of GIF animation assets that are preloaded on first launch. We recommend running it from a solid-state drive (SSD) and closing other resource-heavy programs.

Q: What should I include when reporting a bug?

A: Please attach a screenshot of the problem and the log file located at %APPDATA%\dreamworld\main.log. This greatly speeds up our ability to diagnose the issue.

常见问题

Q：游戏无法启动 / 闪退？

A：请依次尝试：
1. 确认 Epic Games Launcher 已更新到最新版本
2. 在游戏库中找到本作 → 点击「…」→「验证」→ 修复完成后重试

Q：我的存档在哪里？怎么备份？

A：存档位于 %APPDATA%\dreamworld\save\。建议定期复制整个 dreamworld 文件夹进行备份，重装系统前务必备份。

Q：成就没有显示？

A：成就数据同步至您的 Epic 账号。请打开 Epic 客户端 → 点击右上角头像 →「个人资料」→「成就」标签查看本作进度，同步可能有几分钟延迟。

Q：游戏卡顿？

A：本作包含大量 GIF 动画素材，首次加载时会预加载资源。建议在固态硬盘（SSD）上运行，并关闭其他占用较高的程序。

Q：反馈 Bug 需要提供什么？

A：请附上问题截图，以及日志文件 %APPDATA%\dreamworld\main.log，这将大大加快我们定位问题的速度。
